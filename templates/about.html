About AI HUB

A simple place to find the AI tools that fit your work.

Why AI HUB exists

New AI tools appear every week and it is hard to know which ones are worth trying. AI HUB keeps them in one organised directory so you can search by need, compare options side by side and save the ones you like.

How it works

  • Browse {{ tool_total }} tools across nine categories, or search by name.
  • Filter by free plan, open source and category, then sort the results.
  • Compare up to three tools on features, pricing, platforms, advantages and disadvantages.
  • Take the quiz for recommendations that match your goal and budget.
  • Create a free account to keep favourites, write reviews and build collections.

About the information

Descriptions are written to be general, and pricing is shown only as free, freemium or paid, without prices. Plans change often, so tools marked "unverified details" should be confirmed on the official website. Ratings only come from reviews written by AI HUB members.
